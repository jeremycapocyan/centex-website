## Routing decisions

- Hiscox: public policy management page requires phone support for credit-card and billing-address changes. Do not label this link an online payment form.
- BTIS DirectPay: only for accounts enrolled in that program. Carrier-billed and agency-billed policies may require a different route. The general BTIS number was previously mislabeled as claims.
- Nationwide Life: use life service/claims and life billing numbers, not auto/property claims.
- USLI: corrected claims to 888-523-5545, option 3; billing portal applies to direct-billed policies.
- Safeco: its current public customer-support page displays Liberty Mutual support and payment links. Keep the Safeco directory name for existing customers and explain the routing.
- HOA: portal confirms billing/payment details; label it a billing/policy portal rather than promising guest payment.
- Travelers: separate personal service and business billing.
- Existing claims contacts from Centex were retained where appropriate. New service/payment contacts are sourced above.

## IPFS premium financing

Centex supplied https://centexinsurance.ipfs.com/ . Browser inspection confirmed the branded Centex insured login and Make a Payment link. The portal states it is for Centex insureds with IPFS premium financing agreements. IPFS is a premium finance provider, not an additional insurance carrier.

[Official branch directory](https://www.ipfs.com/en/locations) says to match the first three letters of the account number. Customer Care & Payments: TXA / Austin 866-412-6701; TXP / Dallas 855-891-2621; TXH / Houston 800-247-6129. Do not assume the Austin branch applies to every Centex customer. Other prefixes should use the official branch directory or their statement.
